This is a working prototype, not the finished app. The faders, panning, solo/mute, presets, looping and count-in are all live — they drive real Web Audio gain and pan nodes, which is exactly how the real player will work.

The stems came out of the pipeline in this repo — four separate SATB parts plus a piano reduction, rendered to audio and mixed live in the browser. Stereo reveals a pan control per part: spatial separation buys up to 18 dB of "release from masking", which is the cheapest way to make one line audible inside a chord.

Tempo is per section, not one global number — Luck Be A Lady really does change from rubato to a driving patter tempo mid-song, and slowing down the fast bit shouldn't slow down the ballad. The two sections below are an honest stand-in — this Bach chorale has no printed tempo change, so they're just "bars 1–11" / "12–22" — but the mechanism is real: tap a section to jump there, drag the transport's Tempo slider and it writes to whichever section the playhead is in, and playback hands off to the next section's stored rate the instant it crosses the bar line, not by ramping. It also resamples the audio here, so it shifts pitch — a prototype shortcut. The real player synthesises from MIDI, where tempo and pitch are independent.
